## QUESTION 6: What do I need to do to get my GAP cert?

**ANSWER:** ✅ STEP-BY-STEP GAP CERTIFICATION GUIDE PROVIDED

**Do You NEED GAP Certification?**
- ✅ **YES if:** Selling to major chains, large wholesalers, food service with strict requirements
- ❌ **NO if:** Farmers market, CSA, small local restaurants (ask them first)

**Year 1 Recommendation:** Focus on farmers market/CSA (no GAP needed). Year 2: Pursue GAP if wholesale grows.

**GAP Certification Process (5 Steps):**

**Step 1: Prepare Your Farm**
- Water testing
- Sanitation plan
- Worker health/hygiene policies
- Pest management records
- Equipment cleaning procedures
- Traceability system

**Step 2: Complete SC-430 Vendor Form (USDA AMS)**
- One-time setup
- Establishes billing account
- URL: https://www.ams.usda.gov/resources/sc430

**Step 3: Submit Audit Request (SC-237A & SC-651 Forms)**
- Request audit service
- Specify audit type ("USDA Harmonized GAP")
- Select nearest audit office

**Step 4: USDA Auditor Visit**
- Announced audit (40-80 hours)
- Unannounced verification visits (2 visits for farms 90+ days old)
- Auditor reviews documents, interviews staff, observes practices

**Step 5: Certification Decision**
- Pass = Certificate issued (valid 3 years, use GAP logo on marketing)
- Corrective action = Address gaps, re-audit
- Fail = Develop plan, retry after improvements

**Cost & Timeline:**
- SC-430 form: FREE
- USDA GAP audit fee: $500-1,200
- Follow-up/re-audit: $200-400
- **Total:** $700-1,600
- **Timeline:** 3-4 months from application to certification

**Contact for Florida GAP Audits:**
- **Phone:** (202) 720-5021
- **Email:** SCAudits@usda.gov
- **Find local office:** https://apps.ams.usda.gov/GAPGHP/reportG05.aspx
