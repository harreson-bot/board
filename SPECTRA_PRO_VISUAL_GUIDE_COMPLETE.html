🎯 Spectra Pro — Visual Trading Signals

1️⃣ BUY IMBALANCE — Support Level

What you see: Green zone = buyers dominated here. Price drops below and bounces back UP into the zone.
  • Green highlighted zone = Support (buyers want to buy here)
  • Wide positive delta bars = aggressive buying at those levels
  • Entry: Buy when price bounces back INTO the zone
  • Stop loss: 2 ticks below the zone extreme
  • Target: Resistance above (POC High) or when zone is fully traded through

2️⃣ SELL IMBALANCE — Resistance Level

What you see: Red zone = sellers dominated here. Price rises above and pulls back DOWN into the zone.
  • Red highlighted zone = Resistance (sellers want to sell here)
  • Wide negative delta bars = aggressive selling at those levels
  • Entry: Short when price pulls back DOWN into the zone
  • Stop loss: 2 ticks above the zone extreme
  • Target: Support below (POC Low) or when zone is fully traded through

3️⃣ ZERO PRINTS — Price Gaps (Unfilled Voids)

What you see: Orange zones = price gaps with zero trades. Price is drawn back to "fill" these voids.
  • Zero print = unfilled price gap (no trades occurred)
  • Small gaps (1-2 ticks) fill FAST = quick reversals
  • Large gaps (4+ ticks) = strong support/resistance
  • Entry: SHORT the bounce OFF gap OR BUY if breakout THROUGH gap
  • Target: When gap fully filled or momentum stalls

4️⃣ DELTA DIVERGENCE — Reversal Warning

What you see: Price candle closes UP (looks bullish) but delta bars are RED and WIDE (sellers dominated). Mismatch = weakness. Wait for next candle to confirm reversal.
  • Bearish Divergence: Price UP + sellers dominated = uptrend weak
  • Bullish Divergence: Price DOWN + buyers dominated = downtrend weak
  • DO NOT enter on divergence candle itself
  • Wait for NEXT candle to confirm direction
  • Entry: On next candle IF it moves in the reversal direction

5️⃣ UNFINISHED AUCTIONS — Wick Trap

What you see: A candle's wick (extreme high or low) had zero response from the other side. Market returns to "complete" the auction.
  • Unfinished HIGH: Wick pushed up, no bids = market returns to SHORT it
  • Unfinished LOW: Wick pushed down, no asks = market returns to BUY it
  • Best setup: Unfinished auctions from PREVIOUS SESSION in early RTH
  • Horizontal line = unfinished level extends right until filled
  • Entry: When price returns to the wick level
  • Be careful of close distance = if candle closed far from wick, auction may be already done

Key Takeaway: These five signals work together. The strongest trades happen when multiple signals align (e.g., divergence + imbalance + unfinished auction).
