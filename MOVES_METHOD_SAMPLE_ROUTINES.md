## MAIN PROGRAM - 15-Minute Daily Mobility Routine

### Philosophy:
- Strengthen joints through full ranges of motion
- Move out of alignment (not just perfect form)
- Develop resilience and real-life movement patterns
- Build body awareness and coordination

### Sample Daily Routine (15 minutes)

#### **Warm-Up (2 minutes)**
1. **Arm Circles** - Full 360° rotations, forward & backward
   - 30 sec each direction
   - Let shoulder find full range

2. **Cat-Cow Spinal Flows** - Gentle undulation
   - 30 sec, flowing smoothly
   - Move head, thorax, spine in waves

#### **Main Workout (11 minutes) - Rotate These 3 Focus Areas**

**OPTION A: HIP MOBILITY FOCUS (Days 1, 3, 5)**

1. **90/90 Hip Stretch Series** (2.5 min)
   - Sit on ground, front leg at 90°, back leg at 90°
   - Hold 30 sec, gently explore range
   - Rotate through 3 angles (forward, middle, back)
   - Switch legs, repeat

2. **Frog Stretch to Deep Squat** (2 min)
   - Knees wide, hands on ground
   - Rock forward and back
   - Progress into deeper squat, let gravity assist
   - 1 minute hold, breathing

3. **Open Book Hip Rotation** (2 min)
   - Lying on side, knees bent
   - Rotate top knee over body, opening hip
   - Follow your knee with eyes
   - 1 min each side

4. **Deep Lunge with Rotation** (2 min)
   - Front knee stacked over ankle
   - Back knee down or hovering
   - Rotate torso toward front leg
   - Explore side-to-side rocking
   - 1 min each side

5. **World's Greatest Stretch Variation** (2 min)
   - Lunge position → rotate open → reach up
   - Move slow and controlled
   - Explore different arm positions
   - Build strength through range

**OPTION B: SHOULDER & SPINE FOCUS (Days 2, 4)**

1. **Band Pull-Aparts (or hands together)** (2 min)
   - Arms at shoulder height
   - Pull hands/band apart, retract shoulders
   - 15 reps, rest, repeat
   - Builds shoulder stability

2. **Shoulder Dislocates** (1.5 min)
   - Imagine drawing a big circle with your shoulders
   - Full 360° rotations
   - 30 sec each direction, 2 rounds
   - Explore end ranges

3. **Lying Thoracic Rotations** (2 min)
   - Lie on side, knees bent
   - Top hand reaches across body, opening chest
   - Follow hand with eyes
   - Breathe into opening
   - 1 min each side

4. **Dead Bug Series** (2 min)
   - Lie on back, arms up, knees bent at 90°
   - Opposite arm & leg extend slowly
   - Build coordination and core stability
   - 1 min each side

5. **Child's Pose to Cobra Flow** (1.5 min)
   - Rock between positions
   - Full spinal extension and flexion
   - Feel mobility building
   - 1 minute of flowing movement

6. **Inchworm with Push-Up** (2 min)
   - Stand, fold forward, walk hands out
   - Do 1 push-up, walk feet toward hands
   - Repeat 8-10 reps
   - Builds full-body strength through range

**OPTION C: FULL BODY INTEGRATION (Day 6)**

1. **Crawl Patterns** (3 min)
   - Forward crawl, backward crawl
   - Lateral crawl, diagonal crawl
   - Build coordination and full-body awareness
   - 2 minutes continuous movement

2. **Bird Dog Progression** (2 min)
   - Start on hands & knees
   - Extend opposite arm/leg
   - Add rotation, add hold
   - Build stability and coordination
   - 1 min each side

3. **Quadruped Rocking** (2 min)
   - On hands and knees
   - Rock hips back to heels, forward to shoulders
   - Explore different hand positions
   - Develop strength through ranges

4. **Turkish Get-Up (slow & controlled)** (2 min)
   - Lie down, roll to sitting
   - Push up to half-kneeling, then standing
   - Reverse the movement
   - Build total body coordination
   - 1-2 reps per side

5. **Glute Bridge Series** (1 min)
   - Bridge position
   - Add rotation, add single leg, add hold
   - Build posterior chain strength
   - 30 sec variations

#### **Cool Down (2 minutes)**
1. **Child's Pose Hold** - 1 minute
2. **Gentle Spinal Twist** - 30 sec each side
3. **Deep Breathing** - 30 sec

## SPLITS PROGRAM - 30-Day Progression
## *Achieving Front Splits (Foundational)*

### Philosophy:
- Build strength AND flexibility
- Progress gradually to avoid injury
- Daily 15-20 minute sessions
- Focus on hip, hamstring, and calf mobility

---

### **Week 1: Foundation Building (Days 1-7)**
*Focus: Open hips, gentle hamstring stretching, ankle mobility*

#### Daily Structure: 15 minutes
1. **Warm-Up (3 min)**
   - Light jogging or jumping jacks - 1 min
   - Leg swings forward/back - 1 min
   - Leg swings side-to-side - 1 min

2. **Main Work (10 min)**
   - **Low Lunge Hold** - 2 min each side
     - Front knee stacked over ankle
     - Back knee down or hovering
     - Feel gentle hip flexor stretch
   
   - **Pyramid Pose** - 2 min each side
     - Forward fold, back leg straight
     - Let hamstring gently lengthen
     - Hands on ground or blocks
   
   - **Half Split** - 2 min each side
     - Front leg straight, back knee bent
     - Keep hips square
     - Fold forward slightly
   
   - **Butterfly Stretch** - 2 min
     - Soles of feet together, knees out
     - Fold forward gently
     - Let gravity assist

3. **Cool Down (2 min)**
   - Child's pose - 1 min
   - Supine hip stretch - 1 min

---

### **Week 2: Deeper Stretching (Days 8-14)**
*Focus: Deeper splits-related stretches, build strength in stretched positions*

#### Daily Structure: 18 minutes

1. **Warm-Up (3 min)** - Same as Week 1

2. **Main Work (13 min)**
   - **Low Lunge with Back Knee Down** - 2.5 min each side
     - Back knee on ground for deeper quad & hip flexor stretch
     - Square hips forward
     - Hands stay elevated (blocks or furniture)
   
   - **Half Split (Deeper)** - 2.5 min each side
     - Back knee down more
     - Fold further forward if comfortable
     - Feel hamstring lengthening
   
   - **Runner's Lunge** - 2.5 min each side
     - Front foot forward, back foot extended
     - Hands on ground or blocks
     - Very close to full splits position
   
   - **Pigeon Pose** (hip opener) - 2 min each side
     - Front leg bent, back leg extended
     - Fold forward
     - Open external hip rotators
   
   - **Prone Hip Flexor Stretch** - 1.5 min each side
     - Lie on belly
     - Pull back knee up toward chest
     - Feel front hip opening

3. **Cool Down (2 min)** - Same as Week 1

---

### **Week 3: Splits Position Introduction (Days 15-21)**
*Focus: Light splits attempts, 80% of full range*

#### Daily Structure: 20 minutes

1. **Warm-Up (3 min)** - Same as before

2. **Dynamic Stretches (3 min)**
   - Forward leg swings - 1 min each leg
   - Side leg swings - 1 min each leg
   - Circular leg movements - 1 min

3. **Main Work (12 min)**
   - **Low Lunge Progression** - 2 min each side
     - Back knee on ground
     - Gently walk hands back slightly
     - Feel hamstring tension increase
   
   - **Splits Attempt (90% range)** - 2.5 min each side
     - Hands on blocks for support
     - Front foot forward, back leg extended behind
     - Front knee still slightly bent (not full splits yet)
     - Breathe into position
     - Use arms to support weight
   
   - **Half Splits with Rotation** - 2 min each side
     - Front leg straight
     - Rotate toward front leg then away
     - Build strength and flexibility
   
   - **Reclined Hamstring Stretch** - 2 min each side
     - Lie on back
     - Grab back of thigh or calf
     - Use strap if needed
     - Bring leg toward torso
   
   - **Wall-Assisted Hamstring Stretch** - 1.5 min each side
     - Lie on back near wall
     - One leg up wall
     - Deepen as comfortable

4. **Cool Down (2 min)** - Gentle stretches

---

### **Week 4: Full Splits Achievement (Days 22-30)**
*Focus: Working into deeper splits, building strength in position*

#### Daily Structure: 20 minutes

1. **Warm-Up (4 min)**
   - Joint mobility - 2 min
   - Leg swings - 2 min

2. **Dynamic Activation (3 min)**
   - Lunges walking - 1.5 min
   - Squats - 1 min
   - Hip circles - 0.5 min

3. **Main Work (11 min)**
   - **Splits Progression (95-100% range)** - 3 min each side
     - Hands on blocks for support
     - Front leg straight, back leg extended
     - Let gravity slowly deepen range
     - Can reduce hand support slightly each day
     - Breathe deeply and relax into position
   
   - **Hold with Arm Variations** - 2 min each side
     - Reach one arm forward
     - Reach both arms forward
     - Try to rotate gently
     - Build strength in stretched position
   
   - **Half Split Strengthener** - 2 min each side
     - Front leg straight
     - Gently pulse into stretches (small movements)
     - Build active flexibility
   
   - **Pigeon Pose (Deeper Hold)** - 1.5 min each side
     - Build hip mobility alongside splits
   
   - **Prone Quad Stretch with Hip Flexor** - 1.5 min each side
     - Balance quad and hip flexor stretching

4. **Cool Down (2 min)**
   - Child's pose
   - Gentle spinal twist

## **Key Principles (Moves Method Philosophy):**

✅ **Strength + Flexibility Together** - Don't just stretch passively; build strength in full ranges

✅ **No Pain, Only Sensation** - Sharp pain = stop. Deep stretch sensation = good

✅ **Consistency Over Intensity** - Daily 15-20 min beats 2x per week 60-min sessions

✅ **Breathe Into Stretches** - Never hold your breath; deep breathing helps relaxation

✅ **Progressive Overload** - Each week, explore slightly deeper ranges or longer holds

✅ **Listen to Your Body** - Rest days are okay; modify as needed

✅ **Real-Life Movement** - Practice reaching, rotating, asymmetrical positions (not perfect form only)
