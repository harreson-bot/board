Business Plan: Specialty Crop Farm

Tomatoes, Lettuce, Strawberries & Onions | 20-30 Acre Farm

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This business plan outlines a 20-30 acre specialty vegetable farm in Alachua County, Florida, targeting three revenue streams: farmers market, CSA, and wholesale restaurants.

Startup Cost: $45,000-65,000
Year 1 Revenue: $20,800
Break-Even: 18-24 months
Year 2 Profit: $27,000-37,000

CROP PLAN (20-30 ACRES)

  • Tomatoes (6 acres): $3,000-6,000/acre, farmers market champion
  • Lettuce/Greens (4 acres): $2,500-4,500/acre, CSA staple
  • Strawberries (4 acres): $4,000-8,000/acre, highest margin
  • Onions (3 acres): $1,500-3,000/acre, year-round sales
  • Other (3 acres): Rotation, experimental crops
