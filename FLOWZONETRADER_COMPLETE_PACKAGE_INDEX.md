## ❓ FAQ

### "Should I trade all 3 strategies at once?"
**Answer:** No. Start with Strategy #1 (ORB) for 1-2 weeks. Once you're consistently profitable, add #2. Once #1 and #2 work, add #3. Complexity increases win rate requirements.

### "Which strategy is best for beginners?"
**Answer:** Strategy #1 (ORB). Why? Clearest setup, highest win rate, tightest stops, shortest hold times. Master this first, then scale.

### "How much money do I need?"
**Answer:** Minimum $10,000 for scalability. Start with $50-100 risk per trade (0.5-1% of account). Prop firms like TakeProfitTrading provide $50k+ accounts.

### "Can I automate these strategies?"
**Answer:** Yes. The Pine Scripts ARE automated. They can trade 24/7 if you leave them running. But starts with manual + alerts (you press enter), then graduate to full automation.

### "What if I lose money?"
**Answer:** Assume you will. Trading is hard. The edge is real (70%+ win rate), but psychology defeats most traders. Rules:
- Max 2 losses in a row → stop trading for 30 min
- Max 2% daily loss → stop trading for the day
- Max 3 consecutive losing days → review strategy

### "How long until I'm profitable?"
**Answer:** 4-8 weeks on paper trading + 2-4 weeks on live micro sizing. If not profitable by week 12, the strategy isn't right for your markets. Minimum 100 trades to validate.

### "Can I use these on different symbols?"
**Answer:** Yes, but scripts are optimized for **liquid futures** (NQ, ES, MNQ, MES). Stocks work but wider spreads = tighter stops required. Avoid illiquid symbols (wide spreads kill these strategies).

### "Should I watch the charts all day?"
**Answer:** No. Set alerts (scripts do this). Check every 30 minutes. The trades only happen during peak hours (9:30-12:00, 1:00-3:00 EDT). Don't stare at charts; let the system work.
