## YOUR PRIMARY REVENUE STREAMS

### 1. FARMERS MARKET (Saturday 8:30 AM-12:00 PM)
- **Location:** You live next door! (HUGE advantage)
- **Revenue:** $400/week (Year 1) → $600-800 (Year 2-3)
- **Setup:** 10x10 booth, arrive by 8:00 AM
- **Fee:** $25-40/week
- **Best Products:** Strawberries, tomatoes, greens (premium pricing)

### 2. CSA (Community Supported Agriculture)
- **Model:** Customers pay $25-30/box weekly
- **Delivery:** Pickup at farmers market Saturday
- **Year 1 Goal:** 15-20 boxes/week = $375-600/week
- **Marketing:** Facebook, email, farmers market sign-ups
- **Revenue Growth:** Add 5-10 boxes every month

### 3. WHOLESALE (Local Restaurants)
- **Target Customers:** Afternoon, Doro, Fresh Kitchen, Ward's Supermarket
- **Price:** 40-50% of retail ($0.50-1.50/lb depending on crop)
- **Minimum Order:** 10-20 lbs per restaurant
- **Delivery:** 2x/week (Tuesday/Thursday)
- **Year 1 Goal:** $300-400/week wholesale (3-4 restaurants)

### 4. PICK-YOUR-OWN / AGRITOURISM (Optional Year 2+)
- **Strawberries:** $10-12/box (customer picks)
- **Tomatoes:** U-pick opportunities
- **Revenue:** $200-400/weekend during peak season
