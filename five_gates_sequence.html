Detailed Exercise Instructions

GATE ONE: ANKLE SPIRALS

Position: Standing, feet shoulder-width apart
Duration: 45 seconds per side (90 seconds total)

Steps:

  1. Shift weight onto one foot
  2. Lift opposite heel off the ground
  3. Trace a slow figure-eight pattern with the raised ankle
  4. Move through every degree of rotation available to you
  5. Complete 10 spirals in one direction
  6. Reverse direction for 10 more spirals
  7. Switch feet and repeat

Coaching Notes:

  • The slowness is the medicine
  • Full amplitude of motion is key
  • This is maintenance, not effort
  • Move through all available rotation

GATE TWO: KNEE CIRCLES

Position: Feet together, knees slightly bent, hands resting lightly on kneecaps
Duration: 50 seconds total

Steps:

  1. Both knees together
  2. Perform slow, deliberate circular rotations
  3. 10 clockwise rotations
  4. 10 counterclockwise rotations
  5. Keep hands on knees throughout (light contact only)
  6. Make the circles wide and deliberate

Coaching Notes:

  • Focus on the full range of the knee joint
  • Keep feet planted
  • Controlled, continuous motion

GATE THREE: HIP CIRCLES

Position: Standing, feet shoulder-width apart, hands on hips
Duration: 60 seconds total

Steps:

  1. Draw the widest possible circles with your pelvis
  2. Engage your entire hip joint through full amplitude
  3. 8 rotations clockwise
  4. 8 rotations counterclockwise
  5. Do not rush it

Coaching Notes:

  • This is the largest fascial reservoir in the body
  • Full amplitude is essential
  • Slow, deliberate, powerful motion
  • Feel the hips opening

GATE FOUR: SPINAL WAVE

Position: Standing freely OR lightly against a wall
Duration: 60 seconds total

Steps:

  1. Begin at the crown of your head
  2. Tuck your chin gently
  3. Round forward vertebra by vertebra through:
    • Cervical spine (neck)
    • Thoracic spine (upper back)
    • Lumbar spine (lower back)
    • Tailbone (sacrum)
  4. Move as if each disc drops along a string
  5. Reverse the wave — start from tailbone and roll upward
  6. Complete 6 full waves

Coaching Notes:

  • This is spinal articulation, not just bending
  • Feel each vertebra moving sequentially
  • Move slowly and deliberately
  • This pumps fluid through intervertebral fascia

GATE FIVE: SHOULDER CIRCLES

Position: Standing, arms at sides
Duration: 50 seconds total

Steps:

  1. 10 slow shoulder rolls backward
    • Maximum range of motion
    • Minimum speed
    • Feel shoulder blades glide fully apart
    • Feel them come fully together on each pass
  2. 10 slow shoulder rolls forward
    • Same principle: maximum range, minimum speed
    • Full shoulder blade articulation

Coaching Notes:

  • This is about shoulder blade mobility
  • Full range of motion is important
  • Slowness amplifies the effect
