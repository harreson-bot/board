Frequently Asked Questions

How does the contact matching work? ▼
Upload a CSV with names, emails, or phone numbers. Our AI enriches the data using Generect API and finds matching social profiles (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn). You see verified contacts with their social handles ready for DM outreach.
Is this TCPA compliant? ▼
Yes. PatchHub includes built-in opt-out management, consent tracking, and messaging templates that follow TCPA and CAN-SPAM regulations. We also maintain a master opt-out list across all campaigns.
How many DMs can I send per day? ▼
Unlimited, but we recommend 50-100 per day per account to maintain deliverability. Our AI paces messages naturally to avoid triggering platform spam filters.
Can I customize DM templates? ▼
100%. Create your own templates with variables like [FirstName], [Company], [Niche]. Our AI personalizes each message and can A/B test variations.
What if I don't have API keys? ▼
No problem. We provide a managed API key service so you don't need to set anything up. Just click "connect" and start enriching contacts immediately.
Is there a free trial? ▼
Yes. 14-day free trial with full access to all features. No credit card required. Cancel anytime.
