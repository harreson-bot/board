📊 The Footprint: What You're Looking At

Left Side = Order Flow (Delta) — The Aggression Indicator

  • Wide bars = Strong imbalance (one side dominated)
  • Positive delta (green/blue) = Buyers were aggressive (lifting offers)
  • Negative delta (red) = Sellers were aggressive (hitting bid)
  • Neutral/small bars = Balanced buying and selling
  • Saturation mode = Darker = stronger imbalance (makes dominant levels obvious)

Right Side = Volume Profile — The Activity Level

  • Widest bar = POC (Point of Control) — most-traded price in that candle
  • Bars inside Value Area = Where 70% of volume traded (most likely support/resistance)
  • POC is a potential pivot — price often bounces here

🎲 Five Core Signals for Entries & Exits

1️⃣ IMBALANCE ZONES — "The Money Print"

What it is: A cluster of price levels where one side (buyers OR sellers) dominated 150%+ more than the other side.

Visual: Colored background zones extending right from the candle.

Signal Setup Entry Exit
BUY Imbalance Active buy imbalance zone (green) + price pulls back INTO the zone Buy on the bounce INTO the imbalance Scale out at first resistance (POC above) OR when imbalance zone is traded through
SELL Imbalance Active sell imbalance zone (red) + price rallies back INTO the zone Short on the rejection at the imbalance Cover at first support (POC below) OR when imbalance zone is traded through

Key Rule:

Buy imbalances are support (price wants to go back up). Sell imbalances are resistance (price wants to go back down).

Pro Tip:

Imbalance zones that extend for 3+ candles without being filled = STRONG level = better entry odds.

2️⃣ ZERO PRINTS — "The Vacuum"

What it is: Price gaps within a candle where NO trades occurred — unfilled price voids.

Visual: Subtle colored bands extending right. Price "skipped over" these levels.

Signal Setup Action
Zero Print Gap Zone appears on chart and extends right unfilled Price is DRAWN to fill these voids. Watch for quick price reversals back into zero print zones.
Zone Gets Filled Candle trades through entire zero print zone Zone invalidated. Often creates strong breakout momentum in that direction.

Entry:

Fade bounces OFF zero prints. If price rejects a zero print level, short the rejection (sellers defending). If price breaks through, ride the momentum (don't fight it).

Pro Tip:

Small zero print gaps (1–2 ticks) fill FAST. Large gaps (4+ ticks) often act as strong support/resistance.

3️⃣ DELTA DIVERGENCE — "The Reversal Warning"

What it is: Price and volume disagree.

  • Bearish Divergence: Price closes UP ↑ but sellers dominated (negative delta) = FAKE BUYING
  • Bullish Divergence: Price closes DOWN ↓ but buyers dominated (positive delta) = FAKE SELLING
Divergence What It Means Action
Bearish (price up, delta down) Uptrend is weak. Buyers are passive. Candle close near highs. PREPARE TO SHORT. Wait for next candle to confirm weakness, then enter short.
Bullish (price down, delta up) Downtrend is weak. Sellers are passive. Candle close near lows. PREPARE TO BUY. Wait for next candle to confirm strength, then enter long.

⚠️ Important:

Do NOT enter on the divergence candle itself. Wait for the NEXT candle to confirm the reversal is real.

Pro Tip:

Divergence + Price touching an imbalance zone = HIGH PROBABILITY reversal.

4️⃣ UNFINISHED AUCTIONS — "The Wick Trap"

What it is: A candle's extreme (high wick or low wick) had zero response from the other side.

  • High wick with zero bids = Buyers pushed price up, sellers didn't show up = Unfinished HIGH
  • Low wick with zero asks = Sellers pushed price down, buyers didn't show up = Unfinished LOW
Unfinished Type Entry Exit
Unfinished HIGH (no bids at wick) Price comes back down to the unfinished high level → SHORT at the level. The market "fills" it by coming back to complete the auction. Cover at nearest support OR when price breaks below support.
Unfinished LOW (no asks at wick) Price comes back up to the unfinished low level → BUY at the level. The market "fills" it by coming back to complete the auction. Scale out at nearest resistance OR when price breaks above resistance.

Pro Tip:

Unfinished auctions from the PREVIOUS SESSION = strong magnets for price in early RTH. These are your best scalp setups.

⚠️ Caution:

If the candle that created the unfinished auction closed FAR from the wick (more than 4 ticks away), the market already rejected it. The auction may already be resolved.

5️⃣ BUBBLE LOTS — "The Volume Cluster"

What it is: Large circles at price levels with extreme volume or extreme delta (imbalance).

Visual: Sized circles overlaid on the footprint. Bigger bubble = more activity/imbalance.

Bubble Setup Action
Large positive bubble (green, upper levels) Strong buying happened here. If price drops below, it's support. If price rallies above, that level may resist on the way back down.
Large negative bubble (red, lower levels) Strong selling happened here. If price rallies above, it's resistance. If price drops below, that level may support on the way back up.
Bubble at POC Most activity of the candle. Strong pivot level. Bounce candidates.

📈 Stat Table — One-Glance Metrics

Recommended Setup:

1st Row: Delta (shows aggressive buying/selling per candle) 2nd Row: Cumulative Delta (running total imbalance) 3rd Row: Volume (see which bars had real activity) 4th Row: Off
  • Red stat = negative (selling dominance)
  • Green stat = positive (buying dominance)
  • Large swings in cumulative delta = directional strength

⚡ Live Trading Flow — Step-by-Step

SCALP SETUP (1–5 minute timeframe, hold 2–10 minutes)

1

Scan for active imbalance zones

on current bar + last 2 bars. Do they extend 2+ bars unfilled? (Extended zones = stronger). Are they at Value Area boundaries? (Higher probability)

2

Look for divergence confirmation

Price near imbalance + delta divergence signal = Enter. Wait for NEXT candle close to enter (don't chase the divergence candle itself)

3

Check unfinished auctions from last 2–3 candles

Are they still unfilled? Is price within 2–3 ticks of returning to the level? If yes → This is your entry zone

4

Confirm with volume

Stat Table should show positive/negative delta in your direction. Cumulative delta should show direction strength (not flattening)

5

Place entry at imbalance zone or unfinished auction level

Stop loss = 1–2 ticks beyond the level (failed breakout). Target = First POC above/below OR opposite imbalance zone

6

Exit triggers:

  • Profit target hit
  • Stop loss hit
  • Imbalance zone fully traded through
  • Opposite divergence signal appears

DAY TRADE SETUP (5–15 minute timeframe, hold 15–60 minutes)

1

Identify session-level imbalance clusters

(zones extending 3+ bars). Buy imbalance = support, sell imbalance = resistance

2

Wait for price pullback into the zone

Check delta profile: Are there ITM or trapped participants at this level? Trapped participants = better odds for momentum continuation

3

Confirm with POC bounce

If price bounces off VAL (support) + positive delta = BUY. If price rejects VAH (resistance) + negative delta = SHORT

4

Entry at POC or just above/below Value Area

Stop loss = Just beyond the opposite extreme of the Value Area. Target = Next major resistance/support OR 10+ ticks profit

5

Manage with zero prints

If price approaches an unfilled zero print zone in your direction, hold. If price enters an unfilled zero print zone against you, exit half position

6

Exit triggers:

  • Profit target hit
  • Stop loss hit (failed bounce)
  • Price breaks through opposite Value Area boundary (breakout mode)
  • Divergence signal appears (weakness in your direction)

❌ Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Entering ON the divergence candle → Wait for next candle to confirm
  • Trading unfilled zero prints without confluence → Always confirm with imbalance or divergence
  • Ignoring stop losses → Always define stops at the opposite extreme of your zone
  • Chasing price into exhaustion → Wait for the bounce, don't chase the move
  • Trading during value area reversals → POC bounces are strongest; avoid fighting them
  • Overleveraging near unfinished auctions → High volatility here; size down
  • Not waiting for delta confirmation → Divergence + negative delta in your direction = best odds
  • Fighting the Value Area → Price stays in VA 70% of the time; breakouts are rare and explosive
