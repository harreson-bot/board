✅ CRITICAL SUCCESS CHECKLIST

  • ✅ Land secured by Month 2 (lease or own)
  • ✅ Water/irrigation figured out (critical in FL)
  • ✅ Farmers market vendor approved by Month 3
  • ✅ FSA pre-approved for loan/financing
  • ✅ Soil tested + layout planned
  • ✅ Connected to 2-3 other local farmers

⚠️ AVOID THESE MISTAKES

  • ❌ Plant before securing markets
  • ❌ Start too big (over 30 acres)
  • ❌ Underestimate labor needs
  • ❌ Ignore irrigation/water access
  • ❌ Go alone (join networks!)
