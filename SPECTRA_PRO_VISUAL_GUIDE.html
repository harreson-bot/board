🎯 Spectra Pro — Visual Trading Signals Guide

1️⃣ BUY IMBALANCE ZONE — Support Level

What you see: Green highlighted zone showing strong positive delta (buyers dominated). Price has fallen below this zone and is now bouncing back UP into it. The zone extends to the right as an "active" unfilled level.
Key Points:
  • Green zone = Support — Buyers want to come back and buy here
  • Wide delta bars = Aggressive buying happened at these price levels
  • Zone extends right = Still active, hasn't been traded through yet
  • Price bouncing back up = Confirmation buyers are defending this level
📈 ENTRY:
Buy when price bounces back INTO the green imbalance zone. Place stop 2 ticks below the zone extreme.
📉 EXIT:
Sell at resistance above (POC High) OR when the zone is fully traded through.

2️⃣ SELL IMBALANCE ZONE — Resistance Level

What you see: Red highlighted zone showing strong negative delta (sellers dominated). Price has risen above this zone and is now pulling back DOWN into it. The zone extends to the right as an "active" unfilled level.
Key Points:
  • Red zone = Resistance — Sellers want to come back and sell here
  • Wide delta bars (negative) = Aggressive selling happened at these price levels
  • Zone extends right = Still active, hasn't been traded through yet
  • Price falling back down = Confirmation sellers are defending this level
📈 ENTRY:
Short when price pulls back DOWN into the red imbalance zone. Place stop 2 ticks above the zone extreme.
📉 EXIT:
Cover at support below (POC Low) OR when the zone is fully traded through.

3️⃣ ZERO PRINTS — Unfilled Price Gaps

What you see: Price gaps up (orange zones) where NO trades occurred. The price "skipped over" these levels. Price then pulls back down, attempting to "fill" these unfilled voids. These gaps act as magnets that the market is drawn back to.
Key Points:
  • Orange highlighted zones = Zero trades happened here (vacuum)
  • Price "wants" to fill gaps = Often bounces or reverses at these levels
  • Small gaps (1-2 ticks) fill FAST → quick reversals
  • Large gaps (4+ ticks) = strong support/resistance, may take longer to fill
📈 ENTRY:
SHORT the bounce when price rejects at zero print level (sellers defend the gap). OR BUY if price breaks THROUGH the gap (strong momentum).
📉 EXIT:
When gap is fully filled (price trades through entire zone) OR momentum stalls.

4️⃣ DELTA DIVERGENCE — Reversal Warning

What you see: Price closes UP (candle body is green or at high), but the delta bars are RED and WIDE (sellers dominated). This is a mismatch — the candle looks bullish but volume is bearish. Classic reversal warning.
Key Points:
  • Bearish Divergence: Price up + sellers dominated = uptrend is WEAK
  • Bullish Divergence: Price down + buyers dominated = downtrend is WEAK
  • Reversal warning, not immediate entry — Wait for NEXT candle to confirm
  • Look for next candle to move in the divergence direction = reversal confirmation
📈 ENTRY:
DO NOT enter on the divergence candle. Wait for NEXT candle. If it closes lower (bearish), then SHORT.
📉 EXIT:
Cover at first support OR when divergence reversal fails.
