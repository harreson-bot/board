**Important Tax Rule:**  
Post-2017 tax law (IRC §274(e)) eliminated general entertainment deductions. However:
- **Meals:** 50% deductible when directly related to business
- **Entertainment/Amusement:** 0% deductible (no longer allowed)
- **BUT:** Equipment you purchase for business use (like hunting gear for your guiding business) may be:
  - Depreciated if it's equipment/inventory
  - Deducted if it's consumable supplies

**Question:** Are these sporting goods:

- (B) Supplies FOR clients? (gear provided to clients, consumed/given away)

If (A): Limited deduction under entertainment rules (mostly non-deductible post-2017)  
If (B): May be deductible as business entertainment supplies

**Action:** Clarify (A) or (B), and we'll determine correct treatment.
