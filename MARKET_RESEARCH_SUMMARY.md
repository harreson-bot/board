## CSA (COMMUNITY SUPPORTED AGRICULTURE) OPPORTUNITY

### CSA Model Explained
Customers pay upfront ($25-30/box weekly), receive seasonal produce boxes.

**Advantages:**
- ✅ Predictable weekly revenue
- ✅ Customer loyalty (they're invested)
- ✅ Less need for fancy packaging
- ✅ Builds email list for direct marketing
- ✅ Flexibility in what you grow

**Challenges:**
- ❌ Must deliver consistently
- ❌ Customer expectations high
- ❌ Liability if product issues
- ❌ Requires marketing/sign-ups upfront

### Your CSA Strategy (Year 1)

**Pricing:**
- $25-30 per box (depending on season & quantity)
- Small box (5-7 items): $25
- Medium box (8-12 items): $30

**Scale:**
- **Start:** 15-20 boxes/week = $375-600 revenue/week
- **Month 3:** 20-25 boxes/week = $500-750/week
- **Month 6:** 25-35 boxes/week = $625-1,050/week

**Content (Seasonal):**
- Spring: Tomatoes, lettuce, greens, onions, herbs
- Summer: Tomatoes, peppers, squash, herbs (limited)
- Fall: Tomatoes, lettuce, greens, onions, root crops
- Winter: Greens, onions, root crops, herbs

**Delivery:**
- Saturday pickup at farmers market (eliminates delivery cost)
- Alternative: Home delivery (extra fee: $3-5)

**Marketing:**
- Farmers market sign-ups (booth table)
- Facebook group (free to create)
- Email list (collect at market)
- Nextdoor app (hyperlocal targeting)
- Word-of-mouth (best customers refer friends)

### Wholesale Getting Started Process

**Phase 1: Research (Week 1-2)**
1. Visit restaurants during non-peak hours
2. Observe menu items (which are vegetable-heavy?)
3. Note restaurant style (who grows for them?)
4. Collect business cards/contact info

**Phase 2: Contact & Pitch (Week 3-4)**
1. Call restaurant during office hours (10 AM - 12 PM typically)
2. Ask for: Chef, produce manager, owner
3. Introduction: "Hi, I'm starting a local vegetable farm. Do you source local produce? Who are your current suppliers?"
4. If interested: "Can I send over some samples?"

**Phase 3: Sample & Negotiate (Week 5-6)**
1. Bring 5-10 lbs of your best produce
2. Ask: "What volume do you need weekly? What's your pricing expectation?"
3. Establish: Delivery schedule (typically 2x/week), order minimums, price per pound

**Phase 4: Delivery & Repeat (Ongoing)**
1. Deliver Tuesday/Thursday (typical restaurant receiving days)
2. Invoice professionally
3. Follow up for feedback, adjust as needed
4. Build relationship (personal touch = repeat business)
