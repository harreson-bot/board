📊 The Footprint Basics

Left Side = Order Flow (Delta)

  • Wide bars = Strong imbalance (one side dominated)
  • Green/Blue (Positive delta) = Buyers were aggressive
  • Red (Negative delta) = Sellers were aggressive
  • Saturation mode = Darker = stronger imbalance

Right Side = Volume Profile

  • Widest bar = POC = Most-traded price in that candle
  • Value Area bars = 70% of volume (support/resistance)
  • VAH/VAL lines = Value Area High/Low (from session profile)

🎲 Five Core Trading Signals

1️⃣ IMBALANCE ZONES — "The Money Print"

What: Price levels where one side dominated 150%+ more than the other.

SignalSetupEntryExit
BUY ImbalanceGreen zone + price pulls back INTO itBUY the bouncePOC above OR zone traded through
SELL ImbalanceRed zone + price rallies back INTO itSHORT the rejectionPOC below OR zone traded through
⭐ Pro Tip: Imbalance zones extending 3+ candles without fill = STRONG level = better odds

2️⃣ ZERO PRINTS — "The Vacuum"

What: Price gaps where NO trades occurred — unfilled voids.

  • Price is DRAWN back to fill these gaps
  • Small gaps (1-2 ticks) fill FAST
  • Large gaps (4+ ticks) act as strong support/resistance
  • Entry: Fade bounces OFF zero prints OR ride breakouts THROUGH them

3️⃣ DELTA DIVERGENCE — "The Reversal Warning"

What: Price and volume disagree.

DivergenceWhat It MeansAction
Bearish (price up, delta down)Uptrend weak — buyers passivePREPARE TO SHORT (wait next candle)
Bullish (price down, delta up)Downtrend weak — sellers passivePREPARE TO BUY (wait next candle)
⚠️ Don't enter ON the divergence candle — wait for NEXT candle to confirm

4️⃣ UNFINISHED AUCTIONS — "The Wick Trap"

What: A candle's extreme (wick) had zero response from the other side.

TypeSetupAction
Unfinished HIGH
(no bids at wick)		Buyers pushed up, sellers didn't showSHORT when price returns to wick level
Unfinished LOW
(no asks at wick)		Sellers pushed down, buyers didn't showBUY when price returns to wick level
⭐ Best setup: Unfinished auctions from PREVIOUS SESSION = strong magnets in early RTH

5️⃣ BUBBLE LOTS — "The Volume Cluster"

What: Large circles at price levels with extreme volume or delta.

  • Green bubble (upper) = Strong buying → BUY pullbacks to this level
  • Red bubble (lower) = Strong selling → SHORT rallies to this level
  • Bubble at POC = Most active level = Strong pivot

📈 Stat Table Setup

Recommended:
  • 1st Row = Delta (buying/selling per candle)
  • 2nd Row = Cumulative Delta (running imbalance)
  • 3rd Row = Volume (activity level)

Reading: Red stat = selling | Green stat = buying | Large swings = directional strength

⚡ SCALP TRADING FLOW (1-5 min, hold 2-10 min)

1. Scan for imbalance zones on current + last 2 bars. Extended zones (2+ bars) = stronger.
2. Look for divergence — Price near imbalance + delta divergence signal.
3. Check unfinished auctions — Are they within 2-3 ticks of current price?
4. Confirm with volume — Stat Table shows delta in your direction.
5. Enter at imbalance zone or unfinished auction level.
6. Stop loss: 1-2 ticks beyond the zone (failed breakout).
7. Target: First POC above/below OR opposite imbalance zone.
8. Exit triggers: Profit hit | Stop hit | Zone traded through | Opposite divergence appears.

📊 DAY TRADE FLOW (5-15 min, hold 15-60 min)

1. Identify session clusters — Imbalance zones extending 3+ bars.
2. Wait for pullback into the zone. Check delta profile for ITM/trapped participants.
3. Confirm with POC — Bounce off VAL (support) + positive delta = BUY. Reject VAH (resistance) + negative delta = SHORT.
4. Entry at POC or just above/below Value Area.
5. Stop loss: Just beyond opposite Value Area extreme.
6. Target: Next major resistance/support OR 10+ ticks profit.
7. Manage: If zero print in your direction → hold. If against you → exit half.
8. Exit triggers: Profit hit | Stop hit | VAH/VAL breakout (trend) | Divergence weakness.

❌ Common Mistakes

  • ❌ Entering ON the divergence candle → Wait for next candle
  • ❌ Trading zero prints without confluence → Confirm with imbalance/divergence
  • ❌ No stop losses → Define stops at zone extremes
  • ❌ Chasing exhaustion → Wait for bounces, don't chase
  • ❌ Fighting Value Area → Price stays in VA 70% of time
  • ❌ Overleveraging near unfinished auctions → High volatility = size down
  • ❌ No delta confirmation → Divergence + delta in your direction = best odds

✅ Setup Checklist

Before you trade:
  • ☐ Identify active imbalance zones (extended = stronger)
  • ☐ Spot unfilled zero prints (price magnets)
  • ☐ Check unfinished auctions from last 2-3 candles
  • ☐ Look for delta divergence signals (weakness)
  • ☐ Confirm direction with Stat Table (positive/negative delta)
  • ☐ Define entry zone and stop loss BEFORE entering
  • ☐ Define profit target (POC, imbalance, or tick count)
