Wholesale Getting Started

  1. Visit 3-5 target restaurants (peak season August-November)
  2. Ask chef: "Do you source local produce? Who are suppliers?"
  3. Bring 5-10 lbs samples of best produce
  4. Negotiate: Price, delivery schedule (2x/week typical), minimums
  5. Deliver consistently, build relationship
