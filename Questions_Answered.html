Q6: GAP Certification?

What do I need to do to get my GAP cert?
Do You Need It?
✅ YES: Selling to major chains, large wholesalers
❌ NO: Farmers market, CSA, small local restaurants

Timeline: 5 Steps
1. Prepare farm (water testing, sanitation plan)
2. Complete SC-430 form (FREE)
3. Submit audit request
4. USDA auditor visit ($500-1,200 fee)
5. Certification or corrective action

Cost: $700-1,600 total | Timeline: 3-4 months
Recommendation: Year 1 focus on farmers market/CSA (no GAP needed). Pursue Year 2 if wholesale grows.
