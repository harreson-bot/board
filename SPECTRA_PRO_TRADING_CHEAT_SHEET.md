# Flow Zone Spectra Pro Footprint — Day Trading & Scalping Cheat Sheet

## Quick Setup for Active Trading

### Essential Settings
```
Ticks Per Level: 2 (NQ/MNQ) or 4 (ES/MES)
Value Area %: 70%
Footprint Left: Delta mode (Saturation color style)
Footprint Right: Volume Bar
Sessions: Adjust for your market (RTH, extended, etc.)
```

---

## THE FOOTPRINT: What You're Looking At

### Left Side = Order Flow (Delta) — The Aggression Indicator
- **Wide bars = Strong imbalance** (one side dominated)
- **Positive delta (colored green/blue)** = Buyers were aggressive (lifting offers)
- **Negative delta (colored red)** = Sellers were aggressive (hitting bid)
- **Neutral/small bars** = Balanced buying and selling
- **Saturation mode** = Darker = stronger imbalance (makes dominant levels obvious)

### Right Side = Volume Profile — The Activity Level
- **Widest bar = POC (Point of Control)** — most-traded price in that candle
- **Bars inside Value Area** = Where 70% of volume traded (most likely support/resistance)
- **POC is a potential pivot** — price often bounces here

## FIVE CORE SIGNALS FOR ENTRIES & EXITS

### 1. IMBALANCE ZONES — "The Money Print"
**What it is:** A cluster of price levels where one side (buyers OR sellers) dominated 150%+ more than the other side.

**Visual:** Colored background zones extending right from the candle.

**For SCALP/DAY TRADING:**

| Signal | Setup | Entry | Exit |
|--------|-------|-------|------|
| **BUY Imbalance** | Active buy imbalance zone (green) + price pulls back INTO the zone | Buy on the bounce INTO the imbalance | Scale out at first resistance (POC above) OR when imbalance zone is traded through |
| **SELL Imbalance** | Active sell imbalance zone (red) + price rallies back INTO the zone | Short on the rejection at the imbalance | Cover at first support (POC below) OR when imbalance zone is traded through |

**Key Rule:** Buy imbalances are support (price wants to go back up). Sell imbalances are resistance (price wants to go back down).

**Pro Tip:** Imbalance zones that extend for 3+ candles without being filled = **STRONG level** = better entry odds.

---

### 2. ZERO PRINTS — "The Vacuum"
**What it is:** Price gaps within a candle where NO trades occurred — unfilled price voids.

**Visual:** Subtle colored bands extending right. Price "skipped over" these levels.

**For SCALP/DAY TRADING:**

| Signal | Setup | Action |
|--------|-------|--------|
| **Zero Print Gap** | Zone appears on chart and extends right unfilled | Price is DRAWN to fill these voids. Watch for quick price reversals back into zero print zones. |
| **Zone Gets Filled** | Candle trades through entire zero print zone | Zone invalidated. Often creates strong breakout momentum in that direction. |

**Entry:** Fade bounces OFF zero prints. If price rejects a zero print level, short the rejection (sellers defending). If price breaks through, ride the momentum (don't fight it).

**Exit:** When price trades through the entire zero print zone OR when it bounces hard off the zone twice.

**Pro Tip:** Small zero print gaps (1–2 ticks) fill FAST. Large gaps (4+ ticks) often act as strong support/resistance.

---

### 3. DELTA DIVERGENCE — "The Reversal Warning"
**What it is:** Price and volume disagree.
- **Bearish Divergence:** Price closes UP ↑ but sellers dominated (negative delta) = FAKE BUYING
- **Bullish Divergence:** Price closes DOWN ↓ but buyers dominated (positive delta) = FAKE SELLING

**Visual:** Label/symbol above/below the candle.

**For SCALP/DAY TRADING:**

| Divergence | What It Means | Action |
|------------|---------------|--------|
| **Bearish (price up, delta down)** | Uptrend is weak. Buyers are passive. Candle close near highs. | PREPARE TO SHORT. Wait for next candle to confirm weakness, then enter short. |
| **Bullish (price down, delta up)** | Downtrend is weak. Sellers are passive. Candle close near lows. | PREPARE TO BUY. Wait for next candle to confirm strength, then enter long. |

**Entry:** Do NOT enter on the divergence candle itself. Wait for the NEXT candle to confirm the reversal is real.

**Exit:** Take profit at first support (for shorts) or resistance (for longs).

**Pro Tip:** Divergence + Price touching an imbalance zone = **HIGH PROBABILITY** reversal.

---

### 4. UNFINISHED AUCTIONS — "The Wick Trap"
**What it is:** A candle's extreme (high wick or low wick) had zero response from the other side.
- **High wick with zero bids** = Buyers pushed price up, sellers didn't show up = Unfinished HIGH
- **Low wick with zero asks** = Sellers pushed price down, buyers didn't show up = Unfinished LOW

**Visual:** Dot at the wick tip. Optional horizontal line extends right.

**For SCALP/DAY TRADING:**

| Unfinished Type | Entry | Exit |
|-----------------|-------|------|
| **Unfinished HIGH** (no bids at wick) | Price comes back down to the unfinished high level → SHORT at the level. The market "fills" it by coming back to complete the auction. | Cover at nearest support OR when price breaks below support. |
| **Unfinished LOW** (no asks at wick) | Price comes back up to the unfinished low level → BUY at the level. The market "fills" it by coming back to complete the auction. | Scale out at nearest resistance OR when price breaks above resistance. |

**Pro Tip:** Unfinished auctions from the PREVIOUS SESSION = strong magnets for price in early RTH. These are your best scalp setups.

**Caution:** If the candle that created the unfinished auction closed FAR from the wick (more than 4 ticks away), the market already rejected it. The auction may already be resolved.

---

### 5. BUBBLE LOTS — "The Volume Cluster"
**What it is:** Large circles at price levels with extreme volume or extreme delta (imbalance).

**Visual:** Sized circles overlaid on the footprint. Bigger bubble = more activity/imbalance.

**For SCALP/DAY TRADING:**

| Bubble Setup | Action |
|--------------|--------|
| **Large positive bubble (green, upper levels)** | Strong buying happened here. If price drops below, it's support. If price rallies above, that level may resist on the way back down. |
| **Large negative bubble (red, lower levels)** | Strong selling happened here. If price rallies above, it's resistance. If price drops below, that level may support on the way back up. |
| **Bubble at POC** | Most activity of the candle. Strong pivot level. Bounce candidates. |

**Entry:** Buy the pullback TO a positive bubble (support). Short the rally to a negative bubble (resistance).

**Exit:** Opposite extreme of the bubble cluster.

**Pro Tip:** Multiple large bubbles stacked = institutional involvement. These levels hold better.

## STAT TABLE — ONE-GLANCE METRICS

**Recommended Setup:**
```
1st Row: Delta (shows aggressive buying/selling per candle)
2nd Row: Cumulative Delta (running total imbalance)
3rd Row: Volume (see which bars had real activity)
4th Row: Off
```

**What to watch:**
- **Red stat = negative (selling dominance)**
- **Green stat = positive (buying dominance)**
- **Large swings in cumulative delta = directional strength**

## VOLUME PROFILE (RIGHT SIDE PANEL) — SESSION CONTEXT

### The Three Key Lines:
- **POC (Point of Control)** = Most-traded price for the session = Pivot
- **VAH (Value Area High)** = Top of 70% of volume = Resistance zone
- **VAL (Value Area Low)** = Bottom of 70% of volume = Support zone

**For scalping:** Price rejection at VAH/VAL = high-probability bounce.

**For day trading:** Price breaks above VAH = breakout (ride it). Price breaks below VAL = breakdown (short it).

---

## DELTA PROFILE (Right Side Panel) — Trapped Participants

**ITM Dot (green)** = Traders winning at this level (likely to defend/take profit)
**Trapped Dot (red)** = Traders caught on wrong side (likely to panic/add to position)

**Trading Edge:** 
- Price near trapped-trader levels = likely momentum continuation (they liquidate in panic)
- Price near ITM levels = likely resistance (they take profit)

## LIVE TRADING FLOW — Step-by-Step

### SCALP SETUP (1–5 minute timeframe, hold 2–10 minutes)

1. **Scan for active imbalance zones** on current bar + last 2 bars
   - Do they extend 2+ bars unfilled? (Extended zones = stronger)
   - Are they at Value Area boundaries? (Higher probability)

2. **Look for divergence confirmation**
   - Price near imbalance + delta divergence signal = Enter
   - Wait for NEXT candle close to enter (don't chase the divergence candle itself)

3. **Check unfinished auctions from last 2–3 candles**
   - Are they still unfilled?
   - Is price within 2–3 ticks of returning to the level?
   - If yes → This is your entry zone

4. **Confirm with volume**
   - Stat Table should show positive/negative delta in your direction
   - Cumulative delta should show direction strength (not flattening)

5. **Place entry at imbalance zone or unfinished auction level**
   - Stop loss = 1–2 ticks beyond the level (failed breakout)
   - Target = First POC above/below OR opposite imbalance zone

6. **Exit triggers:**
   - Profit target hit
   - Stop loss hit
   - Imbalance zone fully traded through
   - Opposite divergence signal appears

---

### DAY TRADE SETUP (5–15 minute timeframe, hold 15–60 minutes)

1. **Identify session-level imbalance clusters** (zones extending 3+ bars)
   - Buy imbalance = support, sell imbalance = resistance

2. **Wait for price pullback into the zone**
   - Check delta profile: Are there ITM or trapped participants at this level?
   - Trapped participants = better odds for momentum continuation

3. **Confirm with POC bounce**
   - If price bounces off VAL (support) + positive delta = BUY
   - If price rejects VAH (resistance) + negative delta = SHORT

4. **Entry at POC or just above/below Value Area**
   - Stop loss = Just beyond the opposite extreme of the Value Area
   - Target = Next major resistance/support OR 10+ ticks profit

5. **Manage with zero prints**
   - If price approaches an unfilled zero print zone in your direction, hold
   - If price enters an unfilled zero print zone against you, exit half position

6. **Exit triggers:**
   - Profit target hit
   - Stop loss hit (failed bounce)
   - Price breaks through opposite Value Area boundary (breakout mode)
   - Divergence signal appears (weakness in your direction)

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ **Entering ON the divergence candle** → Wait for next candle to confirm  
❌ **Trading unfilled zero prints without confluence** → Always confirm with imbalance or divergence  
❌ **Ignoring stop losses** → Always define stops at the opposite extreme of your zone  
❌ **Chasing price into exhaustion** → Wait for the bounce, don't chase the move  
❌ **Trading during value area reversals** → POC bounces are strongest; avoid fighting them  
❌ **Overleveraging near unfinished auctions** → High volatility here; size down  
❌ **Not waiting for delta confirmation** → Divergence + negative delta in your direction = best odds  
❌ **Fighting the Value Area** → Price stays in VA 70% of the time; breakouts are rare and explosive

## CHEAT SHEET TLDR

**BEFORE YOU TRADE:**
1. Identify imbalance zones (green = buy support, red = sell resistance)
2. Spot unfilled zero prints (price magnets)
3. Check for unfinished auctions from last 2–3 candles
4. Look for delta divergence signals (weak buying/selling)

**ENTRY SIGNALS:**
- BUY: Price bounces into green imbalance + positive delta divergence + POC support
- SHORT: Price rejects red imbalance + negative delta divergence + POC resistance

**EXIT SIGNALS:**
- Profit: First POC or opposite imbalance zone
- Stop: 2 ticks beyond the zone extreme (failed breakout)
- Panic: Opposite divergence signal or momentum shift in delta

**MANAGE WITH:**
- Volume Profile (VAH/VAL for breakout levels)
- Delta Profile (ITM/Trapped for momentum vs. pullback)
- Stat Table (confirm direction with positive/negative cumulative delta)
