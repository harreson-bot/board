❓ CRITICAL: Before Filing, Clarify the $1,000

Is the $1,000:
  • A) Repairs/Maintenance? (oil changes, brakes, tires, etc.)
    → Then it's a deduction, NOT added to basis
    → Depreciable basis stays at $4,060
  • B) Capital Improvements? (major repairs, new engine, transmission, etc.)
    → Then it's added to basis
    → Depreciable basis becomes $5,060
⚠️ This makes a big difference in 2025 depreciation amount!

📊 DEPRECIATION METHOD & CALCULATION

Step 1: Determine Asset Class

Asset Type Light Truck (under 10,000 lbs GVWR)
MACRS Recovery Period 5 years
Depreciation Method 200% Declining Balance (DB) switching to Straight Line
Convention Half-Year (HY) — assumes placed in service mid-year

Step 2: 2025 Depreciation Calculation

For year 1 (2025), using 200% DB-HY method:

Scenario Depreciable Basis Depreciation Rate 2025 Depreciation

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • WRONG: Depreciating on Calvenn's return instead of Caylen's
  • WRONG: Adding repair/maintenance costs to the depreciable basis
  • WRONG: Using straight-line depreciation instead of 200% DB-HY
  • WRONG: Not applying the half-year convention for a 2025 acquisition
  • WRONG: Forgetting to file Form 4562 to claim depreciation
