- ✅ Does NOT require itemizing

**2. Qualified Business Income (QBI) Deduction (IRC §199A)**
- ✅ 20% deduction of qualified business income (if taxable income qualifies)
- ✅ Insurance agent income typically qualifies UNLESS income exceeds $191,950 (single)
- ✅ For 2025: Could save ~$12,000+ in taxes if income < $191,950
- ✅ Already calculated on your 2024 return (but at $0 due to losses)

**3. Deferred Compensation Strategy**
- ✅ Solo 401(k) — Contribute up to 25% of net profit ($25,000/year if profit is $100K)
- ✅ SEP-IRA — Easier to set up, up to 25% of net profit
- ✅ Reduces taxable income AND provides retirement savings
